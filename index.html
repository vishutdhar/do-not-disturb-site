“Noted for the record.”

Headache.

Somebody wasn’t feeling it? Log a headache with the official excuse: Too tired, The show was too good, Leg day, or Feet were cold. Literally. Then the other one of you rules on it: upheld, or overruled.

The rivalry.

Three title belts, held until taken: Instigator in Chief, Reigning Headache Champion and Flame Keeper. When a belt changes hands, the app holds a ceremony. Once.

The reports.

Pick a month, a quarter, six months or a year. The count and its vibe, sessions per week, streaks, prime time, who makes the first move, and the awards for the period.

Your year.

One fact per card, ending on a summary you can share. Shared images carry names and numbers only: never your notes, and nothing about where.

No pressure.

No goals, no targets, no reminders. A streak is a trophy, never a debt.

Private by design.

No account, no server, no analytics. Optional iCloud sync on your own Apple Account. A PIN if you want one.
